# Lotus Imports

Lotus Imports are a trade-only supplier of artificial and silk flowers, based in Rye, East Sussex, and established for over 25 years. Lotus design and hand make all their own bouquets, hand ties, floral and potted arrangements in-house, to order, for florists, retailers, venue dressers and event and wedding planners across the UK.

This organisation holds the code behind the Lotus online shop and internal tools, maintained by [Neuron Drift](https://neurondrift.co.uk).
